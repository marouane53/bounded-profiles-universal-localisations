# Optimality and boundary certificates

Run `python3 -B verify_connective_obstruction.py` or `python3 -B verify_marking_boundary.py` here. Both use only the Python standard library. The top-level `../run_all_checks.py` is preferable for a complete output comparison, because it works in a temporary copy.

## Manuscript results

Theorem 3.13 and Corollaries 3.14–3.16 are proved by localisation, t-structure and finite-projective-duality arguments. Their universal assertions are not computational tests. Proposition 3.17 is proved using Serre duality.

`verify_connective_obstruction.py` checks:

- the degree-minus-two graded-symmetric Frobenius pairings of Example 3.18;
- the six-dimensional Schur algebra of Example 3.19, its complete endomorphism complex, strict dg map, and vertex-component cohomology;
- the analogous sixteen-dimensional Schur algebra for the exterior algebra on two degree-minus-one generators;
- the corrected shifted Hom benchmark for two-term gluings;
- an eighteen-dimensional non-formal tensor control, its differential, cohomology and Massey-product indeterminacy;
- independent rank reductions over F2, F3 and F101.

The last three controls extend the computational discussion in Appendix E; they are not additional all-connective realisation theorems. The helper `verify_nonformal.py` is the same two-term helper used in part 3.

`sphere_schur_multiplication.json` and `exterior_schur_multiplication.json` give bases and multiplication tables and are regenerated by this script. `verification_output.txt` is its captured standard output.

`verify_marking_boundary.py` checks the four-dimensional product algebra and the entire endomorphism dg algebra of Example 3.20. It also computes the image of a degree-zero simple to exhibit failure of t-exactness. `marking_boundary_output.txt` is the captured output. This example separates a full bounded profile from standard t-exactness, and a prescribed dg model from its dg Morita class.

All constructions use exact arithmetic. Field-independent conclusions rely on the manuscript proofs, not on the finite list of test fields.
